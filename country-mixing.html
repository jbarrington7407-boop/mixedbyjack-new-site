Country is what I mix every week

I'm currently the primary front-of-house engineer for a national country act, and I've mixed front of house for Lonestar and worked shows for Josh Turner. Country lives and dies by the vocal and the story, and that's where every one of my mixes starts.

In the studio I've mixed country for Jakota Wass. Send me your raw multitracks and I'll build a mix that keeps the song front and center, with as many revisions as it takes.

How it works

  1. Send your tracks

    Fill out the project form and share a link to your raw multitrack WAV files (Dropbox, Google Drive, or WeTransfer). Export every track from the same starting point so everything lines up.

  2. First mix in 7 days

    I listen through, get a feel for the song, and send you the first version of your mix within 7 days.

  3. Revisions until you're happy

    Send me notes and I'll keep refining it. There's no cap on revisions. We keep going until it sounds the way you heard it in your head.

  4. Get your final WAV

    Once you sign off, you get one finished WAV file per song, ready for mastering or release.

Questions

How long does a mix take?

You'll get the first version of your mix within 7 days of sending your files.

How many revisions do I get?

As many as you need. I'll keep making changes until you're happy with the mix.

What files do I need to send?

All of your raw multitrack files as WAVs, exported from the same starting point so they line up. You can share them through Dropbox, Google Drive, or WeTransfer.

What do I get back?

One finished WAV file for each song.

Do you work with artists outside the US?

Yes. Everything happens online, and I've worked with artists across six continents.

Is mastering included?

Mixing and mastering are booked separately, but they're cheaper when you bundle them together.
